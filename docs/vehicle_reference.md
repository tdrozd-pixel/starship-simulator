# Vehicle reference: Starship V3

Public information as of Nov 2026. SpaceX internal data is not public; anything not from a public source is an estimate. Every vehicle number used in code lives in a parameters file with a comment giving its source (link, estimate, or derived).

- **Super Heavy:** 33 Raptor 3 engines; three grid fins (down from four on V2), each about 50% larger, mounted lower to reduce hot-staging heat, with actuators inside the fuel tank and catch points for the tower.
- **Ship:** six Raptor 3 engines (three sea-level, three vacuum), four flaps, hexagonal tile heat shield.
- **Integrated hot staging:** no disposable interstage; the Booster forward dome takes the Ship's plume directly (protected by tank pressure and steel shielding); interstage actuators retract after separation.
- **Fuel transfer tube:** redesigned so all 33 engines can light simultaneously.
- **Pad:** two separate quick disconnect systems instead of one.

## Numbers used by the sim

The hop configuration (Ship on its three sea-level Raptor 3s, partial propellant load) and every number it uses, with sources and estimates marked, are in `sim_vehicle/include/sim/vehicle/HopperParams.hpp`. Highlights:

- Raptor 3: 280 tf sea-level thrust and 350 s Isp from SpaceX's Aug 2024 performance card (SpaceX later said the 350 s is the vacuum Isp of the sea-level engine; some pages list 250 tf). Throttle 40 to 100%.
- Raptor Vacuum (RVac), three on the Ship's outer ring, fixed (no gimbal). SpaceX does not publish their numbers; every one is an estimate in `HopperParams.hpp`: 250 tf vacuum thrust, 380 s vacuum Isp, 2.4 m nozzle exit, 40% minimum throttle, ring radius 3.6 m with azimuths offset 60 deg from the sea-level engines. The sim refuses to run an RVac (and shuts it down) while the ambient pressure is above 12 kPa (estimate from the Summerfield flow separation criterion), so the hop never lights them. Their mass is in the dry mass.
- Ship: about 52 m long, 9 m diameter, 1600 t propellant capacity. Dry mass is not published; the sim uses 150 t (estimates range from about 100 t to 166 t).
- Landing target: Starbase Pad 2 at 25.996603 N, 97.158251 W (Launch Library 2, third-party), placeholder for the tower catch point.
- Catch tower (build order item 40): everything here is an estimate, in `sim_vehicle/include/sim/vehicle/TowerParams.hpp` and `HopperParams.hpp`. The real Ship has no grid fins and no catch points (the Booster has them), and the Ship is not planned to be caught by arms in the way the sim does it. The sim gives the Ship two hypothetical catch pins 30 m above the base and 7 m either side of the axis (0.25 m radius; a 2.5 m stub outside the 4.5 m skin, long enough that the closed arm tips, whose inner corners are about 5.7 m from the axis, clear the hull by about 1.2 m; at 5 m the closed arm tips intersected the hull by about 1.5 m) so the tower and the catch logic can be built and tested. The tower: a 12 m square, 145 m tall mast at 60 m east of the pad center with two 16 m arms hinged 8 m either side of the approach axis, rail top 75 m, opened 20 deg outward, servo 5.5 deg/s (about 5.7 s to close with the 7 m pin offset, closed angle 4.8 deg inward). The catch window (arm closure, pin position, sink rate, speeds, tilt, heading, angular rate; speeds measured at the pins) and the tower trigger (pin midpoint within 6 m of the seat point and descending, below 45 m above the seat or predicted to reach it within 8 s) are placeholders. The Ship translates to a point over the seat point (about 42.0 m east of the pad center) while high, then descends vertically between the open arms on a 1.5 m/s^2 braking profile (sink rate sqrt(0.5^2 + 2 * 1.5 * height above the seat)); the arms start closing about 50 m above the seat and are closed with about 2 s to spare before the pins touch the cradles at about 0.6 m/s. The hull clears the closed arms by about 1.2 m, so a lateral error above that is a hull strike (ArmStrike).
- Navigation: public sources describe GNSS plus inertial navigation, with tower-side radar reported for the catch. RTK-grade GPS is reported by one unverified source; the sim defaults to RTK and has a standalone preset.
